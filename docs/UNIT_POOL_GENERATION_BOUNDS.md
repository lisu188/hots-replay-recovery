# What recorded unit generations imply about omitted allocations

In the recovered build-98348 ordinary unit pool, retiring an occupied slot increments its generation by one. Allocations use the current generation. The exhausted sentinel is unavailable to ordinary allocations. For two observed allocations in the same slot, generations `a < b` require at least `b - a` retirements and `b - a - 1` intervening complete lifetimes absent from the recorded births. This bound applies within one ordinary pool epoch. It assumes that neither a restore nor a nonstandard producer assigned the generation directly.

Across fifteen stock 98348 recordings, 40,608 gaps between pairs of recorded births require **228,876** intervening lifetimes under this rule. Every previous unit in these intervals has a recorded death. Another 2,320 lifetimes before the first visible use of a slot require the additional assumption that each slot started at generation one. The earliest closed example starts with the diagonal blocker in slot 138, generation 1. It dies at loop 610; Chromie's time trap is born in slot 138 with generation 3 at loop 748. An intervening generation-2 allocation and retirement are necessary. Each selected interval is tested by constructing a minimal history with the recovered ordinary pool model. This per-slot witness does not recover unrelated units or the interleaving across slots.

For TEN the corresponding counts are 15,657 between explicit births and 228 before the first observed use. These are conditional projections of the 98348 rule to build 41810. Slot 95 goes from a pathing blocker (generation 1, death at loop 610) to a beetle (generation 5, birth at loop 849), leaving three intervening generations. The footman, second Dragon Knight, ranged minion, Monstrosity, fourth Dragon Knight and mercenary knight require four, one, three, six, zero and 31 unreported lifetimes respectively on their observed source slots. A death timestamp bounds the observed history; an engine retirement operation is a distinct event whose exact loop is unresolved.
