# Primary hero identity across respawns

The build-98348 GameLib registers the opening unit in the player record and exports its type and player ID via the PlayerSpawned statistic. The ordinary respawn timer reads that same player record, revives the existing unit and emits the respawn event with its handle. Custom respawn, no-respawn and AbnormalRespawn paths require separate evidence. Special summons and decoys do not inherit the primary hero identity. The normal path establishes handle persistence without assigning the numeric opening index. [Function anchors and replay evidence](../observations/primary-hero-persistence.json)

Across 24 build-98348 controls, 27 lobby selections matched the eight hero types in TEN. All 63 primary deaths used their opening index and recycle value. Each PlayerDeath statistic followed its corresponding unit death by one game loop. All 51 completed revivals reused the opening pair. Twelve primary units were dead at the end. Each of the eight hero types had at least one complete cycle. No primary index was allocated to another observed birth. TEN itself had 47 deaths, 45 revivals on the opening pairs and two heroes dead at the end.

Run `python verify_primary_hero_persistence.py --library GameLib.galaxy --case REPLAY SHA256` with any number of `--case` arguments. The verifier pairs an exact owner, type and loop from PlayerSpawned with a unique unit birth. It then compares the complete physical tracker with the player death statistics and reports deviations. Identical revival handles do not establish when the converted simulation will issue the death or use the timer.
