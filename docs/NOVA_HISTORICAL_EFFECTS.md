# Historical Covert Ops and the observed target motion

TEN's replay header, base and data builds are 41810. Its details member dates the match to **2016-04-12 UTC**. The preserved NGDP snapshot for all six regions was committed March 30. Nova selected Covert Ops at loop 133, with the named award at 134. She selected Triple Tap at 7060, with the award at 7061. [Historical versions snapshot](https://github.com/mdX7/ngdp_data/blob/7508618ff97997fe89c34b5489bbeca6385865f9/US/hero/41810/versions), [March 29 Blizzard patch](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20063493/heroes-of-the-storm-patch-notes-march-29-2016).

Blizzard's original description allowed a one-second uncloaked grace period. The February 2016 update raised the gain to two percentage points per cloaked second. The March 16 update set the base slow to 40% and the cap to 60%. The author-labelled b41810 Nydus export agrees on the gain, cap and grace and lists 65 mana and 2.25 seconds, but its base ability tooltip reports 30%. The later January 2017 redesign introduced the fully charged, free-mana cast. [January 2015 original](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/17345414/heroes-closed-beta-patch-notes-january-13-2015), [February 2016 live notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/19995509/heroes-of-the-storm-patch-notes-february-2-2016), [March 2016 balance notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20057110/heroes-of-the-storm-balance-update-notes-march-16-2016), [Nydus export](https://github.com/nydus/heroesjson/blob/bd1723601cc1a7c05e9417bf76b7fe10297e7ba8/heroes.json), [January 2017 redesign](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/20407400/heroes-of-the-storm-patch-notes-january-5-2017).

In October 2016 Blizzard fixed the fully stacked slow persisting throughout the match after the talent was selected. The report gives no introduction date. A later note in November documents an additional correction to the slow. These issues leave exact March/April implementation and cast-time state unverified. The three SC2Mapster-family trees share the same earliest Nova XML commit from January 2018, which has a five-second activation timer. Earlier December 2016 trees of nydus/heroesjson, nydus/storm-extract, pixelmixr/heroes-data, AeroCross/brawler and AlexBuleon/HotsData yielded no Nova XML. The original map's HeroesData dependency points back to an installed mod. No authenticated pre-2017 CTalent/behavior XML was recovered. [October fix](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20307801/heroes-of-the-storm-balance-update-october-12-2016), [November fix](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20372990/heroes-of-the-storm-patch-notes-november-15-2016), [earliest shared Nova XML](https://github.com/SC2Mapster/SC2GameData/blob/95a4988d9135d0842233e7deec006a8b5da8bc6b/mods/heroesdata.stormmod/base.stormdata/GameData/Heroes/NovaData/NovaData.xml).

In TEN, Nova issued 24 unit-target commands with ability link 378, seven before her heroic choice, and five with link 279, all later. Across 23 independent exact-build controls, seven Nova players in six matches issued 226 link-378 commands, including 87 before their heroic choice. No other player in this cohort issued link 378. These observations and the archived ability forms support the Pinning Shot association. The replay carries numeric links.

Within 36 loops after each link-378 command aimed at a player-owned opening Hero unit, compare consecutive one-loop x/y snapshots from the same observer. Each snapshot is carried by a physical command or unit update. Distinct coordinate chords are deduplicated across observers per issue. At 16 loops/s and 4096 fixed-point units per map unit, the reference speeds for 40% and 60% slowing of a base-4.4 hero are 2.64 and 1.76. The comparison tolerance is 0.02. [Blizzard's base speed](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23057727/heroes-of-the-storm-balance-patch-notes-july-10-2019).

All five TEN chords in the 1.76 band follow the loop-8412 issue against Tyrande, at offsets 18, 19, 23, 24 and 30. Her apparent rates are 1.753–1.757. The same tag outside all its observed link-378 windows has 55 one-loop chords near 4.4 and two near 1.76. In the independent controls the 2.64 band persists for three consecutive same-observer loops in 24 non-Covert and three Covert issues. None of the sample's intermediate 2%-step speed bands has a three-loop run. The data corroborate the two magnitudes. Sparse, potentially curved snapshots and other movement effects leave the exact impact time, stacking, grace and any actual persistent slow defect unresolved.
