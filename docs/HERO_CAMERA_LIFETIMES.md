# Dragon Shire hero respawns and camera references

TEN_GREYMANE has 165 camera fields referring to its ten primary heroes. The initial lobby and the historical layout's 172 opening units give the ten proposed opening tags. The first three independent build-41810 references establish the ordinary death timer at every observed level from 1 to 23. TEN uses levels through 21 at death. One level-3 and one level-22 observation support those individual levels. Thirty-three Murky intervals, twelve Tyrael intervals and five other intervals with fractional seconds are kept outside the whole-second reference curve. [Reference observations](../observations/historical-hero-timers.json)

For each player, take the loop of the player death statistic minus one. Look up the last reported player level at that loop and add sixteen game loops per second of respawn time. The observation period ends at the death of an opening KingsCore. On TEN this reconstructs 47 deaths and 45 revives exactly, checked against separate unit events for all ten opening tags. Tyrande's final timer would expire at loop 19529, eight loops beyond core death. The model transfers recorded player combat and level statistics. Computing those statistics from the original inputs remains open.

The ordinary build-98348 Galaxy timer expiry calls the hero respawn routine, which retrieves the stored player hero and calls UnitRevive on that handle. See GameLib lines 6861–6870 and 17317–17327. The standard delay table appears at HeroesLib lines 4566–4595. Applying those stock delays to TEN reproduces 12 of the 45 recorded revives. Blizzard documented the mid-to-late game change. [April 2026 patch](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24261475/heroes-of-the-storm-live-patch-notes-april-20-2026)

The sparse camera file holds 165 little-endian pairs of 32-bit member offset and 32-bit projected endpoint, for 1,320 bytes total (SHA-256 `26a378ab887eb8dee880e7c88125ab14581b66bcdb4846246cc025ea6b6b0b38`). Independent unit events confirm identity and the complete lifetime on every emitted hero. In TEN, 139 fields follow the hero's first revive, 164 occur during a live interval, and one coincides with a unit transition. The projection preserves that boundary without assigning within-loop order. All 165 words match the separately generated retained-tracker member. Six map-authored fields from the earlier projection are disjoint. The historical-layout target projection assumes the ten standard opening allocations and the recovered ordinary timers.

On fifteen genuine 98348 Dragon Shire replays, the stock model and the separate spawn/lifetime observations agree for 121 of 150 lobby slots through the comparison cutoff. Restricting output to fully matched lifetimes produces 1,331 fields across those replays. This qualification handles special hero spawn and death mechanics by withholding mismatched lifetimes.
