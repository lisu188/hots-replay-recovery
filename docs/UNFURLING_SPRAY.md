# Brightwing's Unfurling Spray

TEN user 0 selected the first Brightwing talent at loop 148, ordinal 914. The tracker recorded `BrightwingUnfurlingSpraySoothingMist` for player 1 at loop 149, ordinal 246. An independent build-41810 replay recorded the same selector and award at loops 88 and 89. An unmodified 98348 replay instead recorded Greater Polymorph at selector 0. The verifier regenerates all six physical selection and award frames. Across the two old games it found 142 and 217 explicit ability orders by Brightwing, respectively. Neither tracker contained a named heal or Mist statistic that identified individual passive pulses or their recipients.

The 41810 hero export specifies a 33.3% range bonus and a passive heal of 96 every four seconds. Blizzard introduced the 33% bonus in 2015, raised the base heal from 96 to 120 in July 2016, and then reduced the talent bonus from 33% to 20%. In September 2017 Blizzard removed the talent and increased the base aura range from 6 to 7. **The 6-to-8 profile is a reconstruction.** The later documented starting value of 6 and the rounded April 2016 percentage support an increment of 2 if the base was unchanged. The two original replays do not measure either absolute radius. The separate UnfurlingSpray map, SHA-256 `bdf99d5bd9610774fe1758855f5c1f7981d9ef126e906cf06fd94177e1d3e72e`, builds on PerfectAim. It restores the old catalog choice in the first hero-tree slot, sets the base healer's rate from 110 to 96 and retains the four-second cooldown. In the reconstructed range profile, the ordinary injured-ally gate and actual healing search both start at 6. The chosen talent adds 2 to each field for the selecting player. The map preserves the existing target filters, the gate's unit-radius extension and the alternate quest branch. It removes the Arcane Flare center-hit call to the healing search which Blizzard added in 2018. The button has the historical icon and English text. The current active cleanse, rank interactions, radius indicators, effective passive recipients and actual per-player validator writes remain outside the verified functionality.
